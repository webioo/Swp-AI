What we're building

Two products, one standard of rigor. Systems that touch compliance and money don't get to be flaky.

Banking support platform

Routing, escalation, and case handling built around approval chains and audit trails, not a generic helpdesk retrofitted for finance. Every case carries a full record of who touched it, when, and under what approval.

Randomized compliance testing

A selection engine for DOT style and workplace testing programs, built so the randomness itself is provable, logged, and unbiased. It's a record you can hand a regulator without hesitation.
